SYRIX CHAT — Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains what information SYRIX CHAT ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. SYRIX CHAT is developed and operated by [Syrix Vision — legal entity name, address, country].

In short: we run our own servers for chat, stories, live streaming and the AI assistant — your messages and the AI assistant's replies are processed on infrastructure we control, not shared with third-party advertising or AI companies. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.

1. Information we collect

Account information: username, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in readable form). Optionally: a profile photo and a short bio you choose to add.

Content you create: chat messages, group/community messages, stories (photos, automatically deleted after 24 hours), and live stream sessions (title, start/end time, viewer count). Messages are stored so you can access your conversation history across devices.

Camera and microphone: only accessed when you choose to start a live stream. Audio/video is transmitted to our self-hosted streaming server to reach viewers; we do not permanently record live streams unless we explicitly tell you otherwise in the future.

Wallet and payments: when you top up your in-app wallet, payment is processed directly by Stripe. We receive and store only the resulting transaction record (amount, credits granted, date) — never your card number, which Stripe alone handles.

AI assistant conversations: messages you send to the in-app SYRIX assistant are processed by an AI model we run on our own infrastructure to generate a reply. They are not sent to a third-party AI provider.

Technical information: basic connection data needed to operate the service (e.g. timestamps, error logs). We do not currently use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the core features of the app: messaging, stories, live streaming, wallet, and the AI assistant.
  • To keep your account secure (authentication, fraud/abuse prevention).
  • To communicate with you about your account (e.g. email verification codes).
  • To comply with legal obligations, including payment record-keeping.

3. Who we share information with

Stripe (payment processing) receives the information necessary to process your payment. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your content with advertisers.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the public.

4. Data retention and deletion

Stories are automatically deleted 24 hours after posting. You can delete your account at any time by contacting us at [support email]; we will delete your personal data except where we are required to retain records (e.g. transaction records for accounting/legal purposes).
